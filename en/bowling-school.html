What Bowling School is

Training on professional lanes, under real playing conditions

Retro Bowling School runs coaching sessions on the lanes of Retro Bowling Club at Partyzantów 22. This is the very venue that hosts the BB Open tournament every year — so you train under the exact same conditions competitors play in.

Whether you're taking your very first steps or you've played recreationally for years and want to raise your level — we'll help you improve your technique, timing and accuracy.

What a session looks like

The coaching program, step by step

1. Grip & stance

The correct ball grip, body positioning and your first safe throws on the lane.

2. Approach & timing

Smooth movement, coordinating your steps with the release — the base of a repeatable throw.

3. Accuracy & reading the lane

Working on your ball path, angle of approach and reacting to pin setups.

4. Rules & scoring

How scoring works, what a spare and a strike are, and how to play tactically across frames.

5. Match-play situations

Drills that mirror real competitive play — including tournament play like BB Open.

6. Group or one-on-one coaching

Sessions matched to group size and goal — recreation or preparing to compete.
