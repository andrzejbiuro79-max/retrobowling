Our story

A bowling alley with soul, inside historic walls

Retro Bowling Club sits inside the atmospheric, historic building of the former "Gręplarnia" wool mill at Partyzantów 22 — on the corner with Michałowicza street. We paired the industrial character of the space with modern bowling equipment to create a place where the whole family has a great time: from a child's first game to an evening with friends at the bar.

It's one of the most modern bowling alleys in Bielsko-Biała — 8 professional lanes with automatic scoring, bumper lanes for the youngest players, pool, darts, and a bar with snacks and cold drinks.

Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to book a lane in advance?

On weekends and evenings we definitely recommend booking by phone — it gets busy. During the week, smaller groups can usually walk in without notice, subject to lane availability.

Is bowling suitable for small children?

Yes — our lanes have bumpers that can be raised, so even the youngest players hit the pins and have a great time.

Do you host corporate events and birthdays?

Yes, we regularly host team-building events, kids' and adults' birthdays, and bachelor/bachelorette parties. Email event@retrobowlingclub.pl to work out the details and a quote.

What's the difference between Bowling School and BB Open?

Bowling School is our coaching program — for beginners and anyone looking to raise their game. BB Open is our yearly, open bowling tournament held on our lanes.
